## Lema: "Take the initiative"
Every organisation needs a motto that represents its mission, vision and values, and its members must abide by it. KICH serves people from low-income communities; whenever we see elderly people, single mothers and children without access to basic needs such as health, education and sustainable development, we don't sit back and watch — we take the initiative to bridge the gap and reduce inequities.

## Director Ejecutivo
Dr. Reginald Kerolle, public health professional and community health advocate in the DR. Founded KICH in November 2007 to provide free health services, prevention and education to remote rural communities in Haiti and the DR.
- Free community-outreach programs in Ascension, Batey Baraguana, La Cabirma, Loma Blanca and Pancho Mateo, serving 1,500+ people each year.
- Educational partnerships with international universities (hands-on clinical experience, global health, mobile clinics).
- Programs: health education, community health clinics, prenatal and pediatric care, HIV/AIDS care, emergency and disaster response, healthcare training, community health internships, adult literacy, counseling and motivational talks.
- Emphasis on social determinants of health and the Sustainable Development Goals.
